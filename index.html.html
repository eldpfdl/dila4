Good to Know

Frequently
Asked Questions

How should I prepare for a Lip Blush treatment?
Apply a thick layer of high-moisture lip balm for at least one week before your appointment. Avoid alcohol, caffeine, and smoking for at least 24 hours before the procedure. Avoid physical lip scrubs and use a warm towel the day before to gently soften the lips. Please arrive without any lip makeup on the day.
What should I avoid before any PMU treatment?
Stop taking blood-thinning supplements (Vitamin E, omega-3, L-arginine) at least one week before your appointment. If you have recently had fillers, Botox, or laser treatments, please let us know. Fillers should be done at least one month prior. Always share any medications, medical conditions, or allergies during your consultation.
What does healing look like after treatment?
Around day 5, the treated area may peel and appear faded. This is completely normal. The final color stabilizes over 6 weeks. Do not pick at peeling skin and keep the area moisturized. Avoid direct water contact for at least 24 hours after the procedure.
I have cold sores. Is PMU safe for me?
Cold sores may appear after lip procedures if you carry the herpes virus. This is a natural immune response, not a complication. We recommend taking antiviral medication (e.g., Acyclovir) as a preventative measure. Please inform us in advance so we can advise accordingly.
How long do PMU results last?
Results typically last 1 to 2 years depending on your skin type, lifestyle, and aftercare. Oily skin tends to fade faster. A touch-up session within 3 months of the initial treatment helps perfect the result and extend longevity.
Is the treatment painful?
We use a low-trauma technique, so most clients experience only minimal discomfort. Topical numbing is applied throughout the procedure. Getting a good night's rest before your appointment will also help. We recommend avoiding appointments during your menstrual period as sensitivity may be higher.
Do you work with all skin types?
Yes. However, very oily skin, keloid tendency, or active skin treatments (e.g., Accutane, steroids) may affect results. If you have pigment residue or discoloration in the treatment area, please send a clear photo without makeup during consultation so we can assess beforehand.
How do I book an appointment?
All bookings are made via WhatsApp. Send a message and we will guide you through a quick consultation and booking form. A deposit is required to confirm your spot. Once received, you will get all appointment details and pre-care instructions.
